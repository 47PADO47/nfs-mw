## Spec firewall record

1. **Spec authors** may read restricted sources for understanding; they wrote what the behaviour *is* (inputs, outputs, state,
   equations, constants, limits) and listed the sources at the top of each spec (verbatim at the end of this page).
2. **Reviewer (2026-11-20)** compared every spec against the rustyAC files above, looking for copied expression (line-by-line
   pseudocode, reproduced comments, identifiers, distinctive layout, wording). Facts (constants, curve shapes, units, behaviour) were
   accepted; the rule is [licensing.md](../licensing.md): "describe the algorithm in our own words". Findings:
   - **Pneumatic trail** (`tyre-force.md` 4.7): the first draft had the same step-by-step structure as the reference (a clamp of
     `1 - 0.8 n`, a cubic, a scale of 1.1 and an offset of 0.1, a factor 0.12, a speed fade). It was **rewritten** as a smoothstep over
     `n` from 0 to `trail_n_end = 1.25` that ends `trail_under = 0.10` below zero. The mathematics is identical (checked to 1e-15 over
     random inputs) and every test vector is unchanged. The constants stay, as facts.
   - **Stability control** (`aids.md` 4.4): the draft followed the reference's three-line yaw-moment computation. It is now stated with
     the mean slip angle of each axle (`|a_rear| - |a_front|`), the same number by a different route; vectors unchanged.
   - Accepted as **facts** and kept: the cubic rise and the hyperbolic fall-off *form* (a textbook brush rise and an asymptotic decay,
     with our own normalised slip and our own blend of the two directions); `fz0 = 2000` (the reference load of every compound of the
     reference game); the peak-slip coupling 0.75 (named `peak_follow`); the relaxation ratios 0.5 (sliding over gripping length) and
     0.3 (load slope), which sit in a multiplicative scheme of ours, not the reference's interpolation; the trail constants 0.12, 1.25
     and 0.10 and the 1 m/s fade; the 0.25 core-to-patch weight and 0.16 psi per degree C; the aid check period and thresholds.
   - No identifier, comment or table layout of the reference appears in the specs.
3. **Implementer** works from the spec alone. If the spec is not enough, fix the spec; do not open the source.

## Implementability check (reviewer, 2026-11-20)

A Python implementation written from the text of `tyre-force.md`, `wheel-dynamics.md`, `parameter-mapping.md` and the appendix of
`scenarios.md` alone reproduced: every vector T1 to T14 of the tyre force; the one-substep table and W1 to W10 of the wheel dynamics;
every row of the parameter mapping; the planar scalars of S1 to S6; and the class table and the 1/144 s check of `targets.md`. Four
spec errors found by doing so were fixed in the specs: the planar car's integration order (the numbers came from an explicit Euler
step, the text said semi-implicit), the split of the drive torque between the two driven wheels, the time stamp convention of
`launch_t100_s`, and the order of the per-axle peak-angle overrides in the parameter mapping.
